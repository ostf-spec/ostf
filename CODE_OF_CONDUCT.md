# Code of Conduct

OSTF welcomes criticism of its draft, implementations and governance. Discuss ideas and evidence directly, and treat the people contributing them with respect.

In repository issues, pull requests and other official OSTF spaces, participants should be constructive, credit others' work and make room for people with different experience. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats and disclosure of private information without consent are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to [conduct@ostf.dev](mailto:conduct@ostf.dev). Include links or other context if you can. Repository maintainers will review reports, limit access to people handling them and may remove content, lock a discussion or restrict participation. A maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome alone.

This policy applies to project spaces and to people representing OSTF in other spaces. It does not require agreement with any technical proposal.
